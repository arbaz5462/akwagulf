Overview

The Leo KBZ submersible pump is engineered for efficient drainage and dewatering in challenging environments. With its heavy-duty construction and advanced design, it performs reliably even when exposed to muddy or slightly sandy water.

Compact yet powerful, the KBZ features high wear resistance and energy-efficient performance — the preferred choice for contractors, engineers and industrial users who need dependable water management.
